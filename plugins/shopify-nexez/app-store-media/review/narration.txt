This is a complete, fresh installation of Nexez Agent-Ready on a Shopify review store. I am starting on Shopify's installation screen before the app has access to the store. The app requests read products, read product listings, and write app proxy. It does not request customer or order data. I will select Install now.

Shopify opens the newly installed embedded app. The status says Account link needed because this store has not been connected to a Nexez account or listing. I will select Continue to Nexez. This user-initiated handoff opens the secure account-link flow outside the embedded frame.

I am signing in with the private reviewer credentials supplied in the app submission. The listing picker shows the exact prepared listing named Shopify Review Catalog 2. I will select that listing and confirm Connect this listing. This choice is explicit, and the app will not silently select another listing.

I am returning to Nexez Agent-Ready in Shopify admin. The Sales channel card now confirms the Shopify channel connection for Shopify Review Catalog 2. Nexez checks the live Shopify channel instead of treating a saved database identifier as proof that the connection still exists.

Next I will select Open products. In Shopify's Manage publishing controls, Nexez appears as Nexez AI discovery. I will publish these two active products to that destination and save the change. Shopify remains the place where the merchant decides which products are available to Nexez.

Back in the embedded app, I will select Sync now. The success message reports the exact number of active products imported from this channel. The latest catalog sync time is now visible. Nexez scopes this reconciliation to the confirmed channel connection, so a product published only to another Shopify destination is not imported.

I will open the agent endpoint. It returns HTTP 200 from the store's app proxy and contains the two products just published. Here is the first product in Shopify admin, and here is the same product in the endpoint. The name, description, variant, price, currency, availability, and original Shopify storefront URL agree.

Now I will change this product's price in Shopify and save it. I will return to the app and run Sync now again. The endpoint now shows the changed price. This demonstrates that Shopify remains the source of truth and that Nexez replaces the synchronized value instead of retaining stale catalog data.

I will also remove the second product from Nexez AI discovery using Manage publishing. After another Sync now, the second product is absent from the endpoint and the first product remains. Unpublishing from this channel therefore removes only the affected Shopify product from Nexez.

For storefront discovery, I will open the theme editor, enable Agent-ready discovery, and save the theme. The app embed publishes discovery links through the configured Shopify app proxy. It does not replace the merchant's theme or add a separate checkout.

From the endpoint, I will follow the remaining product's action. It opens the original product page on this Shopify storefront. Nexez does not process the purchase or divert the buyer to another cart. Shopify remains the checkout and merchant-of-record surface.

Finally, Manage plan in Shopify opens Shopify App Pricing. Plan selection and app charges remain inside Shopify. Returning to the embedded home shows the exact connected listing, a Shopify-confirmed sales channel, and the latest successful sync time. This completes installation, account linking, publication, synchronization, storefront discovery, and checkout verification from start to finish.

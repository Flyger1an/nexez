Nexez Agent-Ready makes a Shopify catalog legible to AI agents while keeping the merchant's storefront and checkout in control. This walkthrough uses a dedicated development store and the prepared Shopify Review Catalog. Reviewer credentials are supplied privately in the app submission, so no password or sensitive account data appears on screen.

After installation, the merchant opens Nexez Agent-Ready directly inside Shopify admin. The embedded home shows the connected store, the selected Nexez listing, and the latest catalog sync. From one clear workspace, the merchant can change the destination listing, refresh products, open the theme integration, or inspect the same endpoint that agents receive.

Connecting a store is always an explicit merchant action. The merchant chooses one of their Nexez listings, then confirms the connection. Nexez does not silently attach a store to an account or restore a previous listing after an uninstall. One Shopify store can be active on a listing at a time, which keeps catalog ownership and attribution clear.

Nexez is registered as a Shopify sales channel. The merchant chooses which active products are published to Nexez using Shopify's channel controls. The catalog sync imports only those channel-published products and preserves the details agents need to compare an offer accurately. That includes product names, descriptions, variants, prices, currency, availability, and the original Shopify storefront URL. A single reconciliation supports up to two hundred and fifty products, and a failed refresh preserves the existing catalog instead of replacing it with incomplete data.

Next, the merchant enables the lightweight Agent-ready discovery app embed in the Shopify theme editor and saves the theme. The embed publishes discovery links through the store's configured app proxy path. It does not replace the storefront, add a competing checkout, or change the merchant's visual theme. It gives compatible agents a stable path to the structured catalog.

Back in Shopify admin, the connected state remains available for routine management. Shopify contextual product-feed events queue a bounded catalog refresh, while the Sync now action gives the merchant immediate control. The app requests read products, read product listings, and write app proxy. It does not request or store customer data or order data.

Plan selection and app subscription charges stay inside Shopify App Pricing. The embedded app opens Shopify's hosted pricing page, then verifies the selected plan through the Shopify Partner API. Shopify-linked accounts cannot create or manage a Nexez Stripe subscription.

The resulting Nexez page presents the business as a concise, agent-readable catalog. The summary exposes the best-fit buyer, offer count, service area, and primary action. The linked agent artifact provides structured product data, and the public page gives human reviewers a clear view of the same catalog. The Open endpoint action returns the artifact body with an HTTP 200 response instead of sending the reviewer through an external redirect.

Each product preserves its Shopify source and shows useful commerce details such as variants, pricing, and availability. The primary action returns the buyer or agent to the original Shopify product page. Nexez does not process the product purchase or divert it to another cart. Shopify remains the system of record for the product and the place where checkout is completed.

To recap, Nexez Agent-Ready is a Shopify sales channel for agent discovery. It imports only channel-published products, keeps plan billing inside Shopify, publishes structured discovery artifacts from the storefront path, and routes product purchases back to Shopify. The merchant retains control of product publication, the listing, storefront, customer relationship, and checkout from start to finish.
